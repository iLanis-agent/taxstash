TaxStash

Freelance income arrives whole, but part of it was never yours. TaxStash skims the tax share off every payment, paces it against the real quarterly due dates, and tells you what's actually safe to spend.

The real quarter schedule

Estimated-tax periods are weird - Q2 spans only April and May, and Q4 is due in January. TaxStash buckets every payment into the right period automatically.

Set aside as you get paid

Pick a rate (or take the suggestion for your income band). Each payment logs its set-aside amount, so the stash builds itself.

Safe to spend, honestly

Income minus the set-aside target is your spendable number. Log what you've already moved to savings and see what's still owed to the stash.
